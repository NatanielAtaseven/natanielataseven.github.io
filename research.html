Rosetta: naming unknown columns in battery‑lab data without a language model

Nataniel Ataseven · Independent project · 2026

When a lab instrument adds a new data column, open-source parsers stop reading the file. Rosetta reads it anyway and names each unknown column from physics, with the evidence. Tested on five frozen hold-outs against Claude Opus 5.5, it gave half as many wrong names and abstained where the data could not support a name.
